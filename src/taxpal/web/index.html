    Checking for WebGPU…

    How the AI works

    One small model sorts your transactions and powers the chat. It runs on your computer's GPU through WebGPU, so your data never leaves this computer. It learns from your corrections by using them as examples; the model itself is never retrained. All tax math is done by TaxPal's rules, not the AI.

    IRS Search Index optional

    Keyword search of the IRS documents works without it. The index adds meaning-based search, so a question like “lunch with a client” also finds the rules on business meals.

    A one-time step that runs a 1 GB model on your GPU.
